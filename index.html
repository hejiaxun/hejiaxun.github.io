Jiaxun He

Robotics · Embodied AI · Planning & Control · Robot Systems

I am a master’s student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, working on robot state estimation, planning and control, embodied intelligence, and real-world robot systems. My work connects physical modeling, learning-based methods, simulation, electronics, and system deployment.

我目前在上海交通大学攻读硕士学位，研究兴趣包括机器人状态估计、规划与控制、具身智能，以及机器人软硬件系统。这个主页用于长期整理公开论文、研究项目和工程作品；内容会随着材料核验与公开边界确认逐步完善。

Research focus

  • State estimation & control: physics-guided learning, tendon transmission, and closed-loop robot validation.
  • Planning & simulation: high-DoF motion planning, controller-in-the-loop simulation, and simulator–measurement calibration.
  • Embodied systems: wearable interfaces, dexterous-hand data collection, human–robot modeling, and Co-design infrastructure.
  • Robot engineering: embedded sensing, motor interfaces, communication, PCB design, and multi-robot integration.

Selected work

  • Physics-Guided Tendon State Estimation: first-author IEEE RA-L paper with real-robot closed-loop validation.
  • DexUMI / XHand Research: public page under preparation; only cleared materials will be included.
  • Constrained-Space Planning for a Cable-Driven Hyper-Redundant Robot: ongoing planning and simulation research.
  • ROBOCON Robot Electronics: PCB, sensing, motor interfaces, and system integration for competition robots.
